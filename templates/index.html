System Architecture

How DeepPrice-MM Makes Pricing Decisions

An end-to-end multimodal deep neural network combining Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and Econometric pricing.

1. Visual Stream (ResNet-18)

Processes 224×224 product images to quantify aesthetics, build quality, and visual packaging premiumness into a 128-dimensional embedding.

2. Text Stream (Sentence-BERT)

Extracts semantic brand strength and technical feature specifications from product titles and descriptions into a 384-dimensional dense representation.

3. Tabular Pricing MLP

Standardizes competitor price benchmarks, cost structure, MSRP, and review ratings through a dense feedforward neural network layer.

Mathematical Formulation

1. Demand Prediction Function:
Q(p) = exp(β₀ - β₁·ln(p) + wᵀ·z_multimodal)

2. Objective Profit Function:
Π(p) = (p - cost) · Q(p)

3. Optimal Price Resolution:
The system evaluates candidate price points numerically to find the global maximum: p* = argmax Π(p).
