DeepPrice-MM: Multimodal Transformer-Driven Dynamic Pricing in E-Commerce via Cross-Attention Fusion of Visual Aesthetics, Textual Specifications, and Econometric Signals

Evaluated under: CSET214 - Data Analysis using Python | Instructor: -Anuj

Abstract

Dynamic pricing in contemporary electronic commerce has traditionally relied upon tabular econometric indicators (competitor price indices, inventory levels, historical transaction velocities), largely overlooking perceptual signals conveyed through product imagery aesthetics and textual marketing copy. In this paper, we propose DeepPrice-MM, a novel multimodal deep learning architecture that fuses visual, textual, and tabular pricing signals using multi-head cross-attention. DeepPrice-MM leverages a ResNet-18 convolutional backbone to capture packaging aesthetics and visual design quality, an all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Transformer encoder to extract semantic feature representations from unstructured product specifications, and a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) for econometric competitor price indices. A multi-head cross-attention fusion network dynamically weighs inter-modal interactions, predicting market unit demand Q(P) and price elasticity with high precision. Evaluated across 1,200 multi-category SKUs with 180 independent test products, our model attains an R² of 0.9368 and a MAPE of 10.18%, substantially outperforming tabular-only (R² = 0.3391), text-tabular (R² = 0.7619), and vision-tabular (R² = 0.6926) ablation baselines. Furthermore, embedding our predictive network into a continuous profit-maximizing optimizer generates a simulated +19.69% net revenue lift over static MSRP benchmarks.

Keywords: Multimodal Deep Learning, Dynamic Pricing, Cross-Attention Fusion, Vision Transformer, Sentence-BERT, Price Elasticity, E-Commerce Optimization.

1. Introduction & Problem Motivation

In competitive digital marketplaces such as Amazon, Flipkart, and Walmart, setting optimal retail prices is critical for balancing sales volume and profit margins. Modern e-commerce platforms feature millions of rapidly updated SKUs, shifting consumer preferences, and algorithmic competitor pricing. Traditional dynamic pricing systems rely predominantly on tabular econometric data, including historical sales volume, competitor scrape rates, stock levels, and categorical discount flags.

However, purchasing decisions in e-commerce are profoundly multimodal. Two items possessing identical technical specs can exhibit vastly differing demand profiles based on high-resolution packaging aesthetics, visual premium cues, and natural language descriptions. Tabular-only pricing systems are inherently blind to these qualitative factors, leading to suboptimal price discovery.

2. Mathematical Formulation & Microeconomic Foundations

We formulate dynamic pricing under a random utility discrete choice framework. A consumer evaluating product SKU i with candidate price Pi derives perceived latent utility:

Ui = α0 + wvT hv(xiv) + wtT ht(xit) + wtabT htab(xitab) - βp · ((Pi / Pcomp) - 1) · γ + εi

The expected market demand volume Qi(Pi) follows a logistic adoption response: Qi(Pi) = Qbase / (1 + exp(-Ui(Pi))). The retailer seeks the profit-maximizing optimal price: P* = argmaxP (P - Ci) · Qi(P).

3. Multimodal Cross-Attention Neural Architecture

The DeepPrice-MM architecture consists of three specialized unimodal feature extraction backbones followed by a Multi-Head Cross-Attention fusion module:

  • Visual Stream: ResNet-18 convolutional backbone mapped to a 128-dimensional aesthetic representation hv ∈ ℝ128.
  • Textual Stream: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Transformer encoder mapping unstructured descriptions to ht ∈ ℝ128.
  • Tabular Stream: 6 econometric market metrics mapped through a 2-layer MLP to htab ∈ ℝ128.
  • Cross-Attention Fusion: 8-head multi-head self-attention layer computing pairwise modality attention weights, followed by residual LayerNorm and dual regression heads (Demand & Elasticity).
